# Query Documentation

## Top Students

Finds highest CGPA students using sorting and limit.

## Department Filter

Filters students by department (e.g., CSE).

## High CGPA

Finds students with CGPA greater than 8.5.

## Age Range

Filters students between ages 20 and 23.

## Skill Search

Finds students who have a specific skill (MongoDB).

## Regex Search

Finds students whose names start with a specific letter.

## Aggregation

* Average CGPA per department
* Student count per department
* High CGPA grouped by department

## Purpose

These queries simulate real-world analytics:

* Ranking students
* Filtering data
* Generating insights
